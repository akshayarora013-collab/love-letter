To the girl who has my heart

If the whole night sky could carry one message to you, I would ask every star to whisper the same thing: you are loved, endlessly and tenderly.

My sweetest love,

Sometimes I wish I could open my heart like a little book and let you read every page, just so you could see how much of it belongs to you. You make ordinary days feel magical. Your smile stays with me long after the moment has passed, and even the thought of being close to you makes the world feel softer.

My love, please take care of your health. Eat properly, drink water, rest when you need to, and never feel that you have to exhaust yourself to do everything. I want you to be gentle with yourself. Your health is precious, your peace matters, and your happiness is something I will always wish for. You don't have to be strong every moment, and you never have to earn my love by pushing yourself too hard.

I want the real moments with you: the silly laughter, the long conversations, the sleepy goodnights, the little updates about your day, the comfortable silences, and the moments when we do absolutely nothing but still feel happy because we're together. I don't need every moment to be extraordinary. With you, even the ordinary becomes my favourite.

My birthday wish · just one thing

Your time, your presence, and a little more of life spent side by side.

No grand gift could mean more to me than making memories with you. 🎂

I don't need anything expensive or perfect for my birthday. The gift I want most is your quality time—your attention, your stories, your laughter, your hand in mine, and those tiny moments that become our favourite memories.

I want to spend every milli-second, every zilli-second, every minute, every little second I can with you. Not to put pressure on you, sweetheart, but because time with you is the kind of gift my heart never gets tired of receiving.

And always remember, my love: please make time for yourself, too. Rest when you're tired, follow your dreams, and look after the beautiful person you are. I want us to keep choosing each other freely—with happy hearts, healthy minds, and love that feels safe.

If I could make one wish and have the universe keep it safe, it would be this: more sunsets with you, more conversations that last too long, more laughter over nothing, more hand-in-hand walks, and more chances to tell you, in a hundred little ways, how much you mean to me.

In every version of tomorrow, in every little second life gives me…
my heart will keep finding its way to you. ♥

Made with a full heart, a little stardust, and all my love ✦
